Spendflo: building a design team and a product from zero

Short form. Case study by Akta Paneri.

{{meta}}

I was Spendflo's first designer. When I joined, the product was a single table for tracking SaaS spend. Over two years I worked with the founders on product direction, hired and led a design team of 3, and set up the design system and processes that helped the team ship 150% faster.

The decision

Drag-and-drop or click-to-create

The first Workflow Builder design was a drag-and-drop canvas. Enterprise buyers expect it, and it looks good in a demo. The Head of Engineering told us that building it properly would add three to six months to a one-quarter deadline. I ran a week of workshops to weigh it up.

Drag-and-drop canvas

3 to 6 months extra, high engineering effort. Familiar, but easy to set up wrongly. Higher demo appeal.

Click-to-create builder Chosen

Within the quarter, low effort. Step by step, hard to break. Lower demo appeal at first.

What I did

Direction, team, one hard trade-off

  • Set a product direction with journey maps and an information architecture that became the quarter-by-quarter roadmap.
  • Hired and mentored the first design team, growing it from 0 to 3.
  • Set up a light design system that stayed in use through the seed and Series A rounds.
  • Ran enterprise research and turned the patterns into reusable workflow building blocks.
