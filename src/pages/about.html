I am Director of Design at Scrut Automation, a governance, risk and compliance (GRC) enterprise SaaS platform. I am based in Bangalore.

I started as a developer and moved into design, and I have more than 15 years of experience across both. I was promoted from Head of Design to Director of Design in July 2026.

I lead a team of 8 designers and strategists. We work in a product, design and engineering trio, organised as pods with floating product managers. I am building an AI-augmented design practice where designers author production code changes.

Before Scrut, I was the founding product designer at Spendflo, taking it from zero to $1M ARR in year one.
